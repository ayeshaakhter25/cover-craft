Job Title: Talent Acquisition Specialist

Job Description:
We are seeking a Talent Acquisition Specialist who doesn’t just fill positions—but identifies potential, builds connections, and brings the right people into the right spaces.

Responsibilities:

Source and attract candidates through multiple channels
Conduct interviews and candidate evaluations
Collaborate with hiring managers on role requirements
Build talent pipelines for future hiring needs
Enhance employer branding strategies

Requirements:

Experience in recruitment or talent acquisition
Strong interviewing and assessment skills
Familiarity with platforms like LinkedIn
Excellent communication and networking abilities
Ability to identify talent beyond resumes
