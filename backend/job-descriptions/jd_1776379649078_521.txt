Job Title: Data Engineer

Job Description:
We are seeking a Data Engineer to design and optimize scalable data pipelines and ensure efficient data flow across systems.

Responsibilities:

Build and maintain ETL/ELT pipelines
Design data models and schemas
Integrate data from multiple sources (APIs, databases)
Optimize query performance and storage
Ensure data quality and reliability

Requirements:

2+ years of experience in data engineering
Strong Python and SQL skills
Experience with tools like Apache Airflow or Apache Spark
Knowledge of data warehouses (BigQuery, Snowflake)
Understanding of data pipelines and distributed systems
