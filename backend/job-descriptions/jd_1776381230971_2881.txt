Job Title: Junior QA Engineer

Job Description:
We are seeking a Junior QA Engineer to ensure the quality and reliability of our software products. The ideal candidate should have a basic understanding of software testing principles and attention to detail.

Responsibilities:

Execute test cases (manual testing)
Identify, document, and track bugs
Work closely with developers to reproduce issues
Perform regression testing
Assist in creating test documentation

Requirements:

Basic knowledge of SDLC and STLC
Understanding of manual testing techniques
Familiarity with bug tracking tools (e.g., Jira)
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Good communication skills
